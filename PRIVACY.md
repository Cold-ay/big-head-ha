# BigHeadHa Privacy Policy | 私隱政策

Effective 21 November 2026 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 21 日

BigHeadHa stores the cards, spending, income and budgets you enter on your device. CSV statements are read on your device. The optional Apple Shortcuts action records the transaction details you choose to pass to it. The app does not require an account, retrieve your Wallet history, or send your financial records to the developer. It contains no advertising, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking.

The app shares the next repayment summary and your amount-visibility preference locally with its Home Screen widget. Removing the app deletes its local records. Any device backups you keep are managed through your Apple settings. Links to external websites open services with their own privacy policies.

If you email support, the developer uses the email address and details you provide to answer your request. Please omit card numbers and bank statements. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the same address. [Contact BigHeadHa support](SUPPORT.md)

BigHeadHa 會將你輸入的卡片、消費、收入及預算儲存在裝置上。CSV 月結單在裝置上讀取。選用 Apple 捷徑時，應用程式只會記錄你傳入的交易資料。使用時無須註冊帳戶，應用程式不會讀取 Wallet 交易歷史，亦不會將財務記錄傳送給開發者。應用程式不設廣告、第三方分析工具或跨應用程式追蹤。

主程式會在裝置上與主畫面小工具共用最近一期的還款摘要及金額顯示偏好。刪除應用程式可移除本機記錄。裝置備份由你的 Apple 設定管理。外部網站適用各自的私隱政策。

如以電郵聯絡支援，開發者會使用你提供的電郵地址及資料回覆查詢。請勿附上卡號或銀行月結單。如需刪除支援通訊，請透過同一電郵地址提出要求。[聯絡 BigHeadHa 支援](SUPPORT.md)
