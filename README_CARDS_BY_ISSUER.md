Sources: [ZA Card](https://bank.za.group/en/za-card), [Mox Card FAQ](https://mox.com/faqs/faq-mox-card/), [Mox Credit FAQ](https://mox.com/faqs/mox-credit/), [Octopus](https://www.octopus.com.hk/en/consumer/octopus-cards/about/index.html).

- Every issuer has an official source link in the bundled catalog. No online refresh, bank connection, reward-rate feed, or automatic exchange-rate download is used.

Preset statement-day/offset values are legacy form suggestions, NOT verified bank terms. New credit cards require the user's first statement date and actual payment deadline. Future deadlines are estimates only. Holidays, posting dates and bank-specific terms may affect the real bill. Recorded HKD spending excludes unrecorded fees, interest, adjustments and bank FX charges.

The original positioning/review text below is historical commentary, not a current benefit guarantee or a complete list of cards available for new applications. Opening an official link is optional and requires internet access; recording remains offline.

This README groups the researched Hong Kong credit cards by issuing company. It includes licensed banks plus specialist/non-bank issuers such as PrimeCredit, AEON Credit Service, and American Express. Benefits change frequently, so use the official card pages as the source of truth before applying.

## Notes on Smaller / Niche Issuers

PrimeCredit and AEON are two relevant smaller/non-bank consumer issuers found in this research. American Express is not a bank issuer in the same way as HSBC or Citi, but it directly issues Hong Kong cards and belongs in the non-bank/specialist group. ZA Bank and Mox Bank are virtual banks; Octopus is included because it is a major Hong Kong spending instrument even though it is not a traditional credit card. Other niche fintech or co-brand issuers may exist, but they were not included unless a current official card catalog with card-face references was found.
